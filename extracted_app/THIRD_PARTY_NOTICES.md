## wordfreq English and Persian Frequency Data

SmartInput's common English protection list is derived from the 30,000 most
frequent English tokens in wordfreq 3.1.1, created by Robyn Speer and
contributors. The generated list is filtered to Latin word tokens.

SmartInput's Persian word-frequency rank (`assets/dictionaries/persian-frequency.txt`,
used by the Contextual priority of Automatic correction) is derived the same way from
the 30,000 most frequent Persian (`fa`) tokens in wordfreq 3.1.1, filtered to
Persian-letter word tokens by `tools/New-PersianFrequencyList.py`.

`assets/dictionaries/persian-common-additions.txt` (common Persian words missing from the
built-in list) is a filtered subset of that frequency list, generated by
`tools/New-PersianCommonAdditions.py`; the same notice and license apply.

The wordfreq software is licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0. Its
frequency data may be redistributed under the Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International license.

Source: https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq
